     File: src/hooks/driver/useDriverOrders.ts
     Risk: HIGH - App can crash with many active bids
     Problem:

     - Driver with 50 active bids = 50 concurrent listeners
     - Memory grows unbounded

     File: src/hooks/client/useClientPayments.ts
     Risk: MEDIUM - Potential payment bypass
